How Renewise Works

From join request to settlement, fully automated

Renewise runs the entire paid-membership lifecycle for your Telegram group or channel — you set the price, everything else happens on its own.

Create / connect your community

Add the Renewise bot to your Telegram group or channel as an admin, then launch the setup wizard from the Mini App or with /createpaywall.

Set membership pricing

Choose a USD price and a billing interval — weekly or monthly. Renewise converts it to TON at payment time using a live rate, locked in on-chain.

Members pay

When someone requests to join, the bot DMs them a payment link — a QR code and a ton:// deep-link — payable from any TON wallet.

Payment is verified automatically

Renewise watches the blockchain, detects the incoming payment, and verifies the exact amount — no manual confirmation, no screenshots, no trust required.

Access is granted automatically

The join request is approved the moment the on-chain payment is confirmed, and the member receives a welcome message.

Renewals are tracked

Members get a renewal reminder before expiry. Paying again extends access — the same vault address is reused, so renewals are instant.

Expired memberships are removed

If a membership lapses after a grace period, Renewise removes the member automatically and keeps your community exclusive.

For Community Admins

Paid membership management on autopilot

Everything a group or channel admin needs to run a paid community — without a payment processor, a custodian, or a spreadsheet.

Automated membership management

Connect your group or channel, set a price, and let Renewise handle join requests, approvals, renewals, and removals. Your community stays exclusive without you lifting a finger.

On-chain payment verification

Every payment is confirmed by watching the TON blockchain — not by trusting a claim. The exact required amount is encoded in a per-subscription smart contract, so verification is deterministic.

Access control & renewals

Join requests are only approved after payment. Renewal reminders go out before expiry, a grace period protects late payers, and expired members are removed automatically.

Direct wallet settlement

Your share of every payment is split on-chain straight to your payout wallet. Wallet changes are protected by a passkey and a 24-hour delay with a cancellation window.

For Developers

Accept TON payments in your own bot or Mini App

The Renewise Payments API lets Telegram bots and Mini Apps integrate crypto payments — no smart-contract knowledge required.

API keys, live & test

Create a platform in the Renewise Mini App and get a publishable/secret key pair for testnet and mainnet. Test keys use free test TON; live keys settle real funds.

Simple payment flow

Create a charge in USD, receive a payment URL and vault address, and send your user to pay. Renewise handles the on-chain split and settlement automatically.

Signed webhooks

Get notified the moment a charge completes. Every webhook delivery is HMAC-SHA256 signed and verifiable, with a delivery log and automatic retries.

Telegram-native integration

Built for Telegram bots and Mini Apps. Use the hosted checkout page, deep-links, and QR codes to onboard users without leaving the Telegram ecosystem.

Non-Custodial by Design

Renewise never holds your funds

Payments are settled by smart contracts on the TON blockchain — not by a Renewise-controlled account.

Per-subscription vault contracts

Each subscription gets its own PaymentVault smart contract, deployed on first payment using TON's deploy-on-first-message pattern. The contract splits the payment atomically in the same transaction: the admin's share goes directly to their payout wallet, the platform fee goes to the platform wallet, and any overpayment is held in the vault for a trustless refund.

Nothing to freeze, seize, or misplace

The split is encoded in the contract at deploy time and cannot be changed retroactively. Renewise has no access to admin or subscriber funds and cannot freeze, reverse, or redirect a completed on-chain payment. A dedicated trigger wallet — holding only a small gas balance — releases overpayment refunds the contract has already validated.
