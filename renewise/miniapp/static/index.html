Terms of Service & Privacy Policy
Please read and accept our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before using Renewise. You only need to do this once — acceptance is shared between the bot and this app.

1. What Renewise Is

Renewise is a Telegram bot and Mini App that lets group and channel admins charge for membership access. Members pay in GRAM (TON). Payments go directly to a smart contract that atomically splits funds between the admin and Renewise's platform fee — no middleman holds your money.

2. Non-Custodial Architecture

Renewise never holds, controls, or has discretionary access to admin or member funds. Completed on-chain splits are final and cannot be reversed. Overpayments above $1.00 USD are detected by Renewise's backend and refunded to the member's TON (GRAM) wallet via a dedicated on-chain trigger, typically within 24 hours.

3. Fees

A 2.00% buyer fee is added to the subscription price paid by the member. A 3.30% admin fee is deducted from the admin's payout. Both fees are always shown to both parties before any payment is made.

4. Pricing & Currency

Admins set prices in USD. The GRAM equivalent is calculated at the live exchange rate at the time the payment link is generated and locked in — it does not change after the link is issued.

5. Subscriptions & Renewals

Renewal reminders are sent 3 days before expiry. A 3-day grace period applies before automatic removal. Comped members are excluded from enforcement.

6. Admin Responsibilities

Admins are solely responsible for the legality and content of their paywalled group or channel, compliance with Telegram's Terms of Service, and the accuracy of their pricing and wallet address.

7. Prohibited Use

Renewise may not be used for illegal content, fraud, intellectual property infringement, or anything that violates Telegram's Terms of Service. Renewise may suspend or ban any account at its discretion.

8. No Warranty

The service is provided "as is." Renewise is not liable for blockchain outages, wallet failures, exchange-rate fluctuations, or third-party service issues.

9. Privacy — What We Collect

We store:

  • Your Telegram user ID, first name, and username
  • Subscription status, renewal dates, and payment transaction hashes
  • For admins: group/channel settings, pricing, and payout wallet address
  • An audit log of all administrative actions

We do not collect private keys, wallet seed phrases, or the content of your Telegram messages outside direct bot interactions.

10. On-Chain Data is Public

Every payment transaction — including amounts and wallet addresses — is permanently and publicly visible on the TON (GRAM) blockchain. This is a property of the blockchain, not a choice Renewise makes.

11. Data Sharing

We share data only with TonCenter (on-chain verification) and CoinGecko (exchange rates). We never sell your data.

12. Your Rights

You may request access to or deletion of your off-chain personal data via support at any time. On-chain data is immutable and cannot be deleted.

13. Age

You must be 18 or older (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction) to use this service.

14. Developer API Additional Terms

If you use the Renewise Payments API to build your own application, you additionally agree to:

  • Secret key security. You are solely responsible for keeping your sk_live_ and sk_test_ secret keys confidential. Never embed them in client-side code, mobile apps, or public repositories. Renewise is not liable for charges or data breaches arising from exposed keys.
  • Payment UX obligation. You must present payments to your users as a ton:// deep-link or QR code. Displaying the raw vault address for manual copy-paste is prohibited. Renewise uses a Deploy-on-first-message smart contract architecture; bypassing the deep-link breaks payment delivery.
  • Telegram environment. The Renewise payment infrastructure is designed and tested for use within Telegram. If you deploy the API outside Telegram, you accept full responsibility for ensuring correct payment UX and user communication.
  • Fair use & rate limits. You must not exceed published rate limits or attempt to circumvent them. Abuse may result in immediate platform suspension without refund.

  • Webhook reliability. If you configure a webhook endpoint, you are responsible for maintaining its availability and correctly verifying the Renewise-Signature header before processing events.

Authentication
Bearer token, dual environments
Every API request must include your secret key:
Authorization: Bearer sk_test_…
The prefix automatically routes to the correct network:

Prefix Network Explorer
sk_test_ TON Testnet testnet.tonscan.org
sk_live_ TON Mainnet tonscan.org

⚠️ Never put secret keys in client-side code, mobile apps, or public repos. Store them as environment variables on your server.
Create a Charge
POST /api/platform/charges
Request body
{
  "amount_usd_cents": 999,   // integer, min 10
  "external_reference": "…" // your order ID, unique/platform
} Response fields
Field Description
charge_id Renewise charge ID
payment_url ton:// deep-link — show this to the user
required_nano_amount Exact nanoTON to send
status pending / completed / expired / failed
Payment UX Critical
Deep-link only. No raw addresses.
🚫 Never show the raw vault_address to users.
Renewise uses a Deploy-on-first-message architecture. The vault smart contract is deployed by the first TON transaction that carries its StateInit payload and that payload is embedded in the payment_url deep-link.

If a user manually copies the raw address and sends from an exchange (Binance, OKX…), the StateInit is missing, the contract does not deploy, and the payment stalls.

Correct approach:
• Telegram Mini App (mobile) embed payment_url in a "Pay with TON" button.
• Desktop / Web render payment_url as a QR code for Tonkeeper / TonHub.

Since Renewise is designed for Telegram, your bot/mini-app should be the delivery mechanism. Using the API outside of Telegram is permitted only when you accept full responsibility for ensuring users never send TON via manual address copy.
Webhooks
Real-time charge events
Set your endpoint URL in the Dashboard → Webhook → Edit. On first setup you'll receive a whsec_… signing secret save it immediately, it's only shown once.

Renewise sends a signed POST to your endpoint for every charge status change:
{"id":42, "external_reference":"order_001",
"status":"completed", "amount_usd_cents":999,
"tx_hash":"abc…def"} Verify the signature before processing:
# Python
import hmac, hashlib
expected = hmac.new(secret.encode(), body, hashlib.sha256).hexdigest()
hmac.compare_digest(expected, header) Return HTTP 200 within 5 seconds. Renewise retries failed deliveries (0 s, 10 s, 30 s). Recent delivery logs are in the Dashboard.
Testing & Sandbox
End-to-end payment simulations
Use the Sandbox tab to test your integration without losing real funds.

🧪 Testnet Mode
Generates simulated charges using your sk_test_ keys. Use a Telegram account connected to the Testnet Wallet to safely test the end-to-end flow.

🔴 Mainnet Mode
To test live Mainnet charges, paste your sk_live_ key into the Sandbox (we don't save it). It simulates real charges but requires real TON to complete.

In both modes, Renewise fires live webhooks to your server immediately upon successful payment detection.

Guide for admins & members
For Telegram Groups & Channels

1️⃣ Add the bot as admin
Add Renewise to your group or channel as an Admin with the required permissions.

2️⃣ Set your price
Choose a USD price and billing interval. Members pay the equivalent amount in TON at the live exchange rate.

3️⃣ Automatic renewal & removal
The bot handles reminders, payments, and kicking unpaid members automatically. Payouts go directly to your wallet.

For Mini App & Bot Developers

1️⃣ Create a Platform
Go to the Developer API section in Settings and create a new platform to get your secret keys.

2️⃣ Integrate the API
Use the Renewise API to generate payment deep-links for your users to pay directly in your app.

3️⃣ Receive Webhooks
Listen for real-time webhooks on your server to instantly deliver digital goods or credits when a user pays.
