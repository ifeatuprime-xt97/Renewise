01 / 14

What Renewise Is

Renewise is a Telegram bot and Mini App that lets group and channel admins charge for membership access. Members pay in GRAM. Payments go directly to a smart contract that atomically splits funds between the admin and Renewise's platform fee no middleman holds your money.

02 / 14

Non-Custodial Architecture

Renewise never holds, controls, or has discretionary access to admin or member funds. Completed on-chain splits are final and cannot be reversed. Overpayments above $1.00 USD are detected by Renewise's backend and refunded to the member's GRAM wallet via a dedicated on-chain trigger, typically within 24 hours.

03 / 14

Fees

A 2.00% buyer fee is added to the subscription price paid by the member. A 3.30% admin fee is deducted from the admin's payout. Both fees are always shown to both parties before any payment is made.

04 / 14

Pricing & Currency

Admins set prices in USD. The GRAM equivalent is calculated at the live exchange rate at the time the payment link is generated and locked in it does not change after the link is issued.

05 / 14

Subscriptions & Renewals

Renewal reminders are sent 3 days before expiry. A 3-day grace period applies before automatic removal. Comped members are excluded from enforcement.
